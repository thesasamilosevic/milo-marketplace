# Weekly Review Question Sets

Ask 3–6 questions at a time, then wait for answers. Choose the set that matches the
review being run.

## If They're Vague or Stuck

- What are the top 1–2 goals you were trying to move forward this week?
- Where did you feel friction most: focus, energy, planning, follow-through, or environment?
- What would make next week a "win" even if it's small?

## Work Alignment

Primary question:

- What actions were out of alignment with your goals and projects?

Prompts:

1. Did you work your protocols to perfection?
2. What do you need to recommit to?
3. What do you need to eliminate or let go of?
4. What caused you to deviate from your protocol?

## Nutrition & Tracking

Primary question:

- How was your nutritional tracking?

Prompts:

1. Review your food diary in [their tracking app — from memory, e.g., Cronometer or MyFitnessPal]
2. Did you stay committed to [their nutrition framework — from memory]?
3. What do you need to recommit to?
4. What do you need to eliminate?
5. What caused you to deviate from your framework?
6. Do you need to adjust your framework?
   - If yes, ensure you:
     - Update [their nutrition dashboard — from memory]
     - Confirm the adjustment meets your targets
     - Confirm the adjustment still hits your micronutrients

## Focus & Execution

Primary question:

- Did you prioritize and focus on your W.I.Ns daily?

Prompts:

1. What got in the way?
2. What caused you to lose focus?
3. How can you prevent this from happening again?
4. What wasn't able to get completed that you planned to complete?
5. What are the reasons this occurred?
6. What will you change this week based on your learnings?
