# SCRIPT CONVERTER

## Purpose

- Take a raw voice memo, transcript, or ramble and shape it into a script the creator can read
- Two modes: long-form (essay or YouTube script) and short-form (45-second reel)
- Keep the creator's personality, style, and voice intact. The job is to organize, not to rewrite them into someone else.

## First step: pick the mode

Ask which output they want if it is not clear:

- Long-form: a short essay or YouTube script. Use Long-Form Mode.
- Short-form: a 45-second reel script. Use Short-Form Mode.

## Constraint (both modes)

Write in active voice and avoid em dashes. Preserve the creator's unique verbiage and tone over polish.

## Long-Form Mode

Rewrite the raw transcript into a short essay or script the creator can read for YouTube. Do not remove details or unique verbiage. Keep their personality, style, and voice intact. The job is only to:

1. Re-organize the thoughts so they flow clearly and logically
2. Eliminate repetition and filler while keeping every key point
3. Format it to be easy to read or speak aloud, using short paragraphs and section breaks
4. Make it engaging for a general audience without oversimplifying

Ask them to paste the raw text, then return the reorganized script.

## Short-Form Mode

Turn the brain dump into a clear 45-second reel script.

Structure it as: hook → setup → three to five key points → strong closing → soft CTA.
