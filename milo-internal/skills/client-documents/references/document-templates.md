# Locked Client-Facing Document Templates

These are approved, locked formats. Follow them exactly when building the document.

## Coaching Agreement (approved May 2026)

Page icon: icons/signature-document_red. Title in all caps (e.g. "MILO COACHING
AGREEMENT"), with the page-name convention applied to the page title itself.

Tone: warm, conversational, second-person ("you"), no contractions, no em dashes,
active voice. No banned words. Closing sign-off is just the name "Saša".

Structure (7 sections):

1. THE ENGAGEMENT: logistics (coach, duration, sessions, rate, payment, between-sessions support).
2. HOW THIS WORKS: the review-based rhythm (prep before, review during, homework after).
3. FOCUS AREAS: 3 areas identified from the discovery call, each with a bold label and description.
4. COACHING ROADMAP: 4 steps as H3 toggle headings, each with prep-work checkboxes and session-agenda checkboxes.
5. WHAT I COMMIT TO: bullet list of coach commitments.
6. WHAT I ASK OF YOU: bullet list of client commitments.
7. FINAL THOUGHTS: short personal closing paragraph.

Formatting details:

- Opening: a personal letter to the client under the H2 header, separated by `---` dividers.
- Section headers: numbered, all-caps H2s (e.g. `## 1. THE ENGAGEMENT`).
- Roadmap sub-sections: H3 toggle headings with `{toggle="true"}`. Format:
  `### STEP 1: TOPIC NAME (Date) {toggle="true"}`. Content inside toggles is tab-indented.
- Each roadmap step has two bold label sections: `**Your prep work (complete before
  Saturday):**` and `**What we cover in the session:**`. All action items are
  checkboxes `- [ ] Item text`. Steps separated by `---` dividers.
- Bold label pattern `**Label:** Value` for engagement details.

Key structural principle: the client does NOT build anything on the call. The client
completes prep work between sessions. Session time is for review, troubleshooting,
and forward direction. Next week's exercises are given at the end of each session,
not sent separately.

## Leader Install Roadmap (approved June 2026)

Deliverable bullet format: a single system or solution name in single apostrophes as
the top-level bullet, with concise one-line sub-bullets listing the elements.

- Example: `- 'Retreat Sales Pipeline'` with sub-bullets like `- Walk through every
  touch from first contact to booked retreat`.
- Phase 1 (Discovery & Friction Audit): named audit deliverables with sub-bullets,
  e.g. `- 'Communication Flow Audit'`.
- Phase 2 (Clear & Install): named systems with sub-bullets for each component, e.g.
  `- 'Coaching Notes System'` with sub-bullets like `- Fathom transcript integration
  into Notion`, `- Organized by rep and meeting type`.
- Phase 3 (Verify & Tune): activity-based bullets (no apostrophe naming) reflecting
  what gets verified and tuned.
- Do NOT use long single-line descriptions that combine the system name and all its
  elements into one bullet. Do NOT start bullets with "We build" or "We clone". Just
  name the system and list what goes into it.

Page structure:

- Callout title: use a universal intro heading, not a repeat of the page title or
  client name. Use `### **C U R R E N T   S T A T E   S U M M A R Y** {color="red"}`.
  This is evergreen and works for any client.
- ROI Math section: bullet points with raw numbers, not paragraph narrative. Format
  `- <span color="red">**Label:**</span> value` for each data point. Include time
  saved, hourly value, weekly savings, monthly savings, subscription cost, return
  multiple, and a "not counted" line for qualitative value. The user talks through
  the math on the call; the page just shows the numbers.
- Investment Summary section: subscription pricing, not one-time install.
  Subscription is $300/month CAD ($3,600/year), or $3,000/year CAD paid annually
  (save two months). Setup fee $500 one-time (monthly) or $250 one-time (annual).
  Keep the 60-day results-based guarantee in this section.
- Named Custom Builds section: same deliverables format (apostrophe naming plus
  sub-bullets), no pricing per item. Intro text: "Projects that surface as your
  system grows. Built collaboratively under your subscription." These are included
  in the subscription, not billed separately.
