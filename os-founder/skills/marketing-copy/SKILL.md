# MARKETING COPY

Write ads and marketing emails by first modeling a proven example, then writing fresh copy to the user's product, audience, and tone.

## First, gather the inputs

Ask for what is missing:

- What is being promoted? (product, offer, link)
- Who is the audience? (role, level, what they care about)
- What tone and voice? (and any copywriter or style to emulate)
- Is there a swipe example to model? If yes, analyze its structure, flow, sequence, and strategy first, and model that, not its content.

## Ad mode

1. If a swipe ad is provided, extract its model: the structure, flow, sequence, and the parts that make it work. Present that model first.
2. Using the model and the inputs, write three ad variations for the target platform (Facebook by default). Use emojis that resonate with the stated audience.
3. Then rewrite the strongest ad, without the structural section labels, into a final form ready to paste.

## Email mode

1. If a swipe email is provided, analyze its structure and strategy and present a reusable model, without using its content.
2. Using the model and the inputs, write an email that promotes the user's product to their list and moves the reader to click through to the sales page.
3. Remove all the structural headings and deliver it in clean, send-ready email form. Use bold and italics to offset the important calls to action. Match the requested tone and language level (for example business-casual for executives), and keep the vibe engaging.

## Constraints

- Write in active voice
- Match the audience's language level: resonant, not stiff
- Lead toward one clear action
