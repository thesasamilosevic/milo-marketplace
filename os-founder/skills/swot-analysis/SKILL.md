# AI-USAGE SWOT

Act as a top AI productivity coach. Produce a SWOT analysis of how the user is using AI in their role.

## First, gather context

Ask the user for:

- Their role and the kind of work it involves
- How they currently use AI (the tasks, decisions, and workflows)
- The recurring weekly or monthly decisions AI touches
- Where AI has helped most, and where it has fallen short

## Output

Deliver a four-quadrant SWOT:

- Strengths: where their current AI use is working and compounding
- Weaknesses: where it is shallow, inconsistent, or creating rework
- Opportunities: five practical use cases where they could use AI (ChatGPT, Claude, and so on) to improve efficiency, creativity, or strategy in their role. Make each specific and actionable.
- Threats: risks of their current approach (over-reliance, quality drift, missed verification, and so on)

Keep it concrete and tied to their actual role. No generic advice.
